[05]method · how i work

Assess. Validate. Report.

Three steps, in the same order, every engagement.

01 / recon

Map the attack surface.

Every engagement starts with mapping what you expose — subdomains, APIs, forgotten endpoints. Attack surface you can't see, you can't defend.

02 / exploit

Exploit, with proof.

Not just "this looks wrong" — a reproducible chain, minimized impact, and the business risk spelled out in plain English.

03 / disclose

Report, and fix it.

Coordinated disclosure only. I stay through remediation — retest, verify, and make sure the fix actually holds.
